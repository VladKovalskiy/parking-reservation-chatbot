# General Information

<a id="overview"></a>
## Overview

Central Parking Garage is a multi-level parking facility for cars,
motorcycles, and bicycles, offering both short-term and long-term parking
through online reservation via this chatbot. The facility is self-service
with automated entry and exit gates; no on-site attendant is required for
normal check-in or check-out.

<a id="contact"></a>
## Contact and Support

For questions the chatbot cannot answer, contact the parking office by email
at support@centralparking.example or by phone during staffed hours. Lost
tickets, access-card replacements, and billing disputes are handled by the
support team, not by the chatbot.

<a id="payment-methods"></a>
## Accepted Payment Methods

Reservations and on-site payments accept major debit and credit cards and
mobile wallet payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay). Cash is not accepted at any
gate or kiosk. A saved payment method is required to complete a reservation.

<a id="vehicle-types"></a>
## Vehicle Types Accepted

The garage accepts passenger cars, motorcycles, and bicycles. Levels 1 and 2
are reserved for cars and motorcycles; a dedicated bicycle rack is available
near the main entrance. Vehicles taller than 2.1 meters cannot enter the
covered levels and should use the open-air overflow lot.

<a id="accessibility"></a>
## Accessibility

Accessible parking spaces are located on Level 1, close to the elevator
lobby. Spaces are reserved for vehicles displaying a valid disability permit.
The elevator and all pedestrian walkways are wheelchair accessible.
