# Booking Process

<a id="how-to-reserve"></a>
## How to Reserve a Spot

To reserve a spot, tell the chatbot your desired date, arrival time, and
expected duration. The chatbot checks availability and proposes a matching
time slot for you to confirm before the reservation is created — no spot is
booked automatically without your explicit confirmation.

<a id="confirmation"></a>
## Confirmation Step

Every reservation requires an explicit "yes" from you after the chatbot
presents the details (date, time, duration, and price). This
human-in-the-loop confirmation step prevents accidental bookings and lets you
review the terms before committing.

<a id="modify"></a>
## Modifying a Reservation

To change the date, time, or duration of an existing reservation, ask the
chatbot to modify it and provide the new details. The chatbot will show the
updated terms and ask for confirmation again before applying the change.

<a id="cancel"></a>
## Cancelling a Reservation

Reservations can be cancelled through the chatbot at any time before the
arrival window starts. The chatbot will confirm the cancellation and state
whether any cancellation fee applies based on how close to the arrival time
the cancellation occurs.

<a id="no-show"></a>
## No-Show Policy

If a vehicle does not arrive within 30 minutes of the reserved start time,
the reservation is released and the space may be given to another driver. A
no-show does not automatically refund the reservation.
