# Rules and Restrictions

<a id="time-limits"></a>
## Time Limits

Reservations can cover a minimum stay of 1 hour and a maximum continuous stay
of 14 days. Longer stays require contacting the parking office directly to
arrange a monthly parking plan.

<a id="prohibited"></a>
## Prohibited Vehicles and Items

Vehicles carrying hazardous materials, trailers, and vehicles over 2.1
meters in height are not permitted inside the covered levels. Storing items,
camping, or living in a vehicle within the garage is prohibited.

<a id="towing"></a>
## Towing Policy

Vehicles parked without a valid reservation, parked outside their reserved
time window, or blocking another vehicle may be towed at the owner's
expense. Towing notices are posted at every entrance.

<a id="guests"></a>
## Guest and Visitor Policy

Visitors must have a reservation made in their own name or one shared with
them by the account holder through the chatbot; the garage does not accept
walk-in parking without a reservation during peak hours.

<a id="liability"></a>
## Damage and Liability

Central Parking Garage is not liable for damage to vehicles or theft of
personal belongings left inside a parked vehicle. Drivers are encouraged to
remove valuables before leaving their vehicle.
