# Location and Directions

<a id="address"></a>
## Address

Central Parking Garage is located at 12 Station Square, in the downtown
district, directly across from the Central Bus Terminal.

<a id="directions-car"></a>
## Directions by Car

From the ring road, take the Downtown exit and follow signs for "Station
Square." The garage entrance is on Station Square itself, on the north side
of the building, immediately after the taxi stand.

<a id="directions-transit"></a>
## Directions by Public Transport

The garage is a 3-minute walk from Central Station's main exit. Bus lines 4,
12, and 27 stop directly outside the Central Bus Terminal, across the street
from the garage entrance.

<a id="entrances"></a>
## Entrances and Exits

Vehicles enter through the north gate on Station Square and exit through the
south gate onto Miller Street. Pedestrians can use either the north or the
east stairwell; both connect to the elevator lobby on Level 1.

<a id="landmarks"></a>
## Nearby Landmarks

The garage sits between the Central Post Office and the Riverside Shopping
Mall, one block from the public library.
